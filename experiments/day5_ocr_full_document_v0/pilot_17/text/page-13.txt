We will end by giving you all something to ponder. When I began my term
as Finch and Softbill Save Director I combed over all the documents col-
lected to date on the Finch and Softbill Save Program. I came across a very
old list created by former FinchSave Directors and committee members of
low priority birds. These birds at the time of that writing were determined
to be well established in US aviculture, and thus deemed low priority. It
has been ten years since that list was created and almost ail of the birds
listed on it are extremely difficult to locate today. In another ten years, will
there be any at all?
