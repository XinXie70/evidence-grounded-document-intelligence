from 0.0069 ppm to 0.20 ppm.

The October 9, 1990, HSI report concluded that the tetrachloroet-
hene soil and ground water contamination detected throughout the
site was probably the result of small-scale spillage of tetrachlo-
roethene over a long period of time. The HSI report recommended
the installation of at least three monitoring wells; however, it
does not appear that any further subsurface investigations have
been conducted at the site as of March of 1991.

RECENT FIELD ACTIVITIES
