Bird Fever or My Aviary
by Paula Hansen, NFSS member
On Valentine's Day in 1991 my husband bought me a male canary from
a local pet shop. Six months after that my youngest daughter, Kim, took my
husband to a restaurant which had a aviary on the outside, built against the

building. She told her dad, “that's what mom wants”. As they ate their bur-
gers and gazed out the windows, the proprietor, Lee Reynolds, gave my

. husband advice. “What ever you build - don't build it cheap!"
