2002 Program Guidelines (what's new):

The Finch and Softbill Save Committee has chosen to establish a multi-tier
program for Finch and Softbill Save. One of, if not the biggest change that
will be made to the 2002 Finch and Softbill Save program will be lifting the
requirement for close banding to qualify for registering with the program.
This will be achieved through establishing a multi-tier program. In the past,
many have voiced an interest in participating in the program but did not
close band their offspring. By establishing a multi-tier program we will
hopefully reach those individuals who would like to join the program.

Tier One:

Tier One of the program will lift the reguirement on close banding. The
Finch and Softbill Save 2002 Program however requires that Tier One par-
ticipants band all their offspring with open (split) numbered bands. You
cannot register same species of bird in both Tier One and Tier Two of the
program. You must choose either Tier One or Tier Two per bird specie.
You may register a certain specie in Tier One and others in Tier Two and
vice versa. The participant upon enrollment will make the decision which
species will be registered in each tier of the program.

Tier Two:

Tier Two of the program will require close banding of all birds registered
with the program. Participants registering with the program will choose
either Tier One or Tier Two for each particular species they are registering
with the program. All offspring produced by Tier Two registered birds
must be close banded.
