The third year we counted 80 birds in the aviary. Cleaning time each week-
end had grown to 4 hours when keeping 80 birds, 2 hours with 40. Cleaning
includes squeegying the windows, water is freshened 3 times a day, and
seed kept in a 5 Ib feeder which hangs in the center of the aviary.

Since then we have kept mixed pairs in the flight, ne more than a 40
birds at one time, one pair per species. Species kept included Hecks Shaft-
tails, Chocolate, fawn, and white Societies, normal Button Quail, Coturnix
Quail, Blue Capped waxbills, Gold Breasted waxbills, AFA Siskin project
birds, red and black headed Gouldians, Java finches, Fire finches, Orange
Weavers, Spice finches, Orange Cheeked waxbills, and Diamond doves.

We found that the best floor covering to use is pine shavings changed
every 2 months or more depending on the population. Our aviary contains 2
florescent fixtures with special UV bulbs. There is a built-in wall heater with
it's own thermostat and hardwired into a separate circuit breaker. Also, there
is a Circulating fan (usually used for bathrooms) on a timer for hot weather
and when it's too dry inside during winter. The fight switch is on the wall
behind the curtain and and there is one in the aviary by the exit deor. An
outside thermometer for monitoring the aviary temperature hangs on the
aviary wall.

We also have a mini microphone {RadioShack} mounted to the ceiling of
the aviary. You can imagine the noise. In the house is a amplified speaker
so we can listen to the birds. Also, outlets are around the aviary room {as
code required) and are used for plug in night fights. This comes in handy at
night with night fright. Both the speaker and night lights really heip with this
problem. The small inside window to the far left opens to an outside double

| wired flight, the flight is off the ground on legs. The door to access the room

is on the far right hand side. Also, past that door is a safe caged walkway
about 8 ft long and 4 wide for preventing oss of birds going out when ser-
vicing the room.

Only one mistake was made in this structure. When pouring the founda-
tion we didn't place any termite plates, which was a problem, but is resolved
now. Note the sink on the left back end. You can hardly see it in this picture.
No more running back and forth for fresh water. Of course it's only cold wa-
ter. Four skylights are in the ceiling Velux which also came with pull shades.
Special expanded metal screens were custom made fer the windows (the
neighborhood cats here weigh in at about 30 Ibs. and were perforating the
regular screens we had.)
