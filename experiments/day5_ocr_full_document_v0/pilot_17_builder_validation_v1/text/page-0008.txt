Group Manager roles:

The 2002 program will continue to incorporate the use of Group Managers.
The Group Managers are responsible for supervising all the Species Captains
in their group of birds and, if no Species Captain is appcinted for a particu-
lar species, responsible for acting as the Species Captain for that species.
They will be directly responsible for providing species profiles for the Finch
and Softbill Save newsletter to the FinchSave Director. Group Managers will
need to work closely with their Species Captains to compile information on
species they are overseeing. We would like to see Group Managers have
repeated breeding success with several birds they are applying to oversee.
(See application form for more information}. The Group Managers will head
the following groups:

Ail Australian Finches

All Waxbills

All African Finches

All Indo-Pacific Finches

All European Finches

All South American Finches
All Softbill Birds

(Please note the groupings listed above are currently being re-evaluated by
the Finch and Softbill Save Committee. Members will be notified of
changes. Do not let our current evaluation of the groupings deter you from
applying for a Group Manager position.)

2002 List of Finch and Softbill Save Excluded Birds:

The 2002 Finch and Softbill Save Committee has reviewed and changed the
current list of excluded birds for the Finch and Softbill Save Program as fol-
lows:

¢ The Shafttail or Heck's Grassfinch will now be incorporated into the
program. At the time when FinchSave was developed the Shafttail
was very well established in U.S. aviculture and had been excluded.
Upon reviewing its status today the Committee feeis that the Shaft-
tail is not as commonly available in U.S. aviculture and would benefit
from being included in the program.

¢ Dove and quail will be excluded from the program at this time. The
committee can review this again at a later date.

¢ The Timor Zebra Finch will be included in the program.
