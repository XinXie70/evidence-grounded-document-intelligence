What YOU can do to make the program more successful:

All Finch and Softbill Save members are entitled to free classified advertising
in the NFSS Bulletin, on our society website (http://www.NFSS.org) and in
the FSS newsletter. We highly recommend that FSS members advertise
their available stock so that fellow FSS members can have access to any
available bloodlines. Presently, the program does not require FSS partici-
pants to sell their offspring to FSS members. We would like however to see
FSS members work together by using our free advertising and offering their
stock to fellow FSS members whenever feasible. Those who would like to
advertise but remain anonymous may contact the FSS Director for more
information.

We encourage ail FSS participants to recruit fellow hobbyists to join the
program. If anyone needs extra program materials to give to an interested
party, please do not hesitate to contact the FSS Director.

Contribute. Contribute. Contribute. If you have time on your hands and
experience with any of the birds included in the program apply for a Species
Captain or Group Manager position.
