Resignations of Two NFSS Panel
Judges

Sadly, NFSS has accepted the res-
ignatious since the last issue of
The NESS Journal of two of our
NFSS Panel Judges - Mr. Clayton
Jones of Soquel. California, and
Mr. Tom Rood of Shelbyville.
Hlinois.

Clayton Jones was voted to the
NFSS Judges Panel in March 1988
and in the 14 years since has
judged finches and softbills at
NFSS shows across the country.
Clayton writes, “With regret. due
to a family illness | must resign my
position as an NFSS Panel Judge.
It has been a great privilege and
experience. 1 wish all my friends
and fellow panel members the best
of fuck." We thank Clayton for his
many years of service to NFSS!

Tom Rood was admitted to the
NFSS Judges Panel in November
1992 and enjoyed busy judging
seasons across the country every
year since. Tom was invited to
judge the Great American Bird
Show in Wisconsin in 2000 and
was our judge at the Natioual Cage
Bird Show in Denver, Colorado in
1997 where Tom had the dubious
distinction of setting the record for
most gag cages presented for judg-
ing at a National!!! Ah. pay-
backs.... But recently Tom made
the decision to part with all of his
personal bird stock. And per Tom,
“Not being actively involved with

the breeding aud exhibiting of the
birds, | do not feel | should remain
on the judges’ panel. Martha, you
know I've always thought a judge
should be actively involved and
keep abreast of the birds and the
hobby. [ know we've had those
discussions in the past. So. I must
keep to my principles and feel I
must remove myself from the
panel. | must say, being at NFSS
judge has been one of the best
highlights of my involvement in
the finch hobby. It's given me the
opportunity to travel places | nor-
mally wouldn't get to go and meet
people I wouldn't have been able to
meet. I did enjoy the judging more
than the breeding and showing. |
know I will miss it.” And how we
will miss Tom!

On behalf of NFSS. thank you,
Clayton and Tom, for all you have
done for NFSS and our hobby!
We'll stay in touch!!!
